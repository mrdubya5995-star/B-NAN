B-NAN Emulator

B-NAN stands for "Beyond Nostalgic Arcade Network"

A small, honest, multi-system game emulator. Import your ROMs, play, save, done — no accounts, no uploads, everything stays on your device.

What's inside

Everything below is real and already working — not a roadmap.

🕹️

41 systems, genuinely playable

NES through N64, PS1/PSP, the full Sega and Atari lineups, Commodore, arcade (MAME/FinalBurn Neo), ZX Spectrum, BBC Micro, Atari 8-bit, and more — every one verified working, not just listed.

🖼️

Automatic box art

Import a ROM and B-NAN matches it against a real box-art database on its own — no manual searching or downloading images.

💾

Save states & save files

Save your progress to a file anytime and load it back in whenever, plus automatic saving on exit and import/export of the actual in-game battery save — take your progress anywhere.

🔒

100% local & private

Your ROMs and saves never leave your device. No accounts, no uploads, no telemetry.

📦

Self-hosted, forever

Emulator cores are bundled directly into the app, not streamed from a CDN — B-NAN keeps working exactly as it does today, even offline.

🗂️

Real library tools

Multi-select bulk delete, search, Favorites and Recents, per-game rename and artwork override — a library that's actually easy to manage.

🖥️

True native desktop apps

Not just a website — real installers for Windows, macOS, and Linux, built with Electron.

🍌

Honest about limits

The in-app Core List says plainly what does and doesn't work yet — no system is claimed as playable unless it actually is.

B-NAN — Beyond Nostalgic Arcade Network. Bring your own legally-owned ROMs; B-NAN doesn't provide or host any game files.
